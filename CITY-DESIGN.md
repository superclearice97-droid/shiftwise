# City workspace — refined simulation

**v0.2.0:** the background is now `src/city-world.js` (renderer) with `src/city-model.js` (pure world model), in monochrome isometric line art. It has a 14×14 block grid of downtown towers, offices, mid-rise housing, parks, an industrial belt and a harbor with cranes, container yards and ships. An elevated two-track metro runs with cosine acceleration and a station dwell; a freight train runs on a ground corridor. There is also road traffic, a turning Ferris wheel with gondolas, and blinking aircraft beacons. Static objects are cached as per-scale sprites, while vehicles, the wheel and lights are drawn live. The "Fig 1 · mm:ss · night" clock and a mono-type train tag (focus view only) echo a technical drawing. Drag/pinch/wheel zoom work in focus view, and pause, reduced motion and hidden-tab suspension still apply. The earlier notes below describe the previous Three.js scene.
