# City workspace — refined simulation

**v0.2.0 (current):** the background is `src/city-world.js` (renderer) with `src/city-model.js` (pure world model). The look follows a reference video the owner supplied: near-black ground (#151619), charcoal building faces, thin mid-grey outlines, and sparse white light. It's an original layout, not a copy of that project.

- **City:** a 14×14 block grid with one main building per block and open ground around it. Building types are long mid-rise slabs with dot-grid windows, offices with floor lines and an occasional lit lobby band, a few tall glass towers (aircraft beacons), and low terminal halls with perforated roofs. There are also palm-lined sidewalks, forest blocks, plazas, parking lots with bay grids, an industrial belt with sawtooth sheds, and a freight line.
- **Waterfront:** a marina with finger piers and moored boats, a rock breakwater, blinking channel buoys, moving boats, plus a container port with gantry cranes and two moored ships.
- **Metro line 1:** an elevated double track with curved station canopies. Trains shuttle Northgate → Central → Millbrook and back, with cosine acceleration and braking and a dwell at each stop. East of the city the line crosses a jagged **gorge** (visible cliff strata, a river on the floor) on a Warren-truss bridge, whose piers are clipped where the far rim hides them.
- **Millbrook village:** ploughed fields with furrows, crops or fallow, farm tracks, hedgerows, gabled houses with a few lit windows, barns, silos and a turning windmill.
- **Rendering:** static objects are cached as per-scale sprites, and ground decals draw first. Vehicles, trains, boats, the windmill and lights draw live. Cars fade at road ends. Drag/pinch/wheel zoom and pan work in Focus view, and panning is bounded to the city, gorge and village. The train tag (focus view only) shows the next station. Pause, reduced motion and hidden-tab suspension still apply. Drawing runs on every requestAnimationFrame callback, and the simulation uses elapsed time, so it's independent of display refresh rate. 120 fps on real hardware is not guaranteed.

The city is decorative. Train speed and status describe the simulation, not live transit or financial data.
