Download Shiftwise for Android or Linux. The downloaded apps work offline and keep your records encrypted on that device, with no account needed.

Download ShiftwiseOpen Shiftwise

Open this page in your device’s browser, outside the ChatGPT in-app browser.

Android

On the downloads page, tap the file ending in .apk. Open it when it finishes, allow installs from your browser if asked, and tap Install.

iPhone · iOS

Open Shiftwise in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, and tap Add.

iPad · iPadOS

Open Shiftwise in Safari. Tap Share (then View More if needed), choose Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if shown, and tap Add.

Linux

Download the .AppImage, allow it to run as a program, and double-click it. Arch and CachyOS users can use the .pacman file, Ubuntu and Debian users the .deb, or anyone can use the .zip.

Ready for offline days

First open and unlock the app while online on each device. Wait until its offline status says it is ready. Your saved records remain encrypted on that device. Changes sync when you reconnect with Shiftwise open and unlocked.

For the same records on another device, sign in to the same account and unlock the same vault. Keep your vault passphrase safe; it cannot be reset to recover encrypted records.

The steps above cover the website. Records in the downloaded Android and Linux apps stay on that device and don’t sync with the website; use an encrypted backup to move them. On iPhone and iPad, Shiftwise is available as a web app only. Clearing browser data can remove unsynced records.
