Privacy, licenses and limitations

Shiftwise v0.2.1 · October 12, 2026. This describes what the app code does; it is not an independent audit or legal advice.

Money information

Shiftwise is a record-keeping and estimation tool, not a bank, brokerage, payroll service or tax adviser. Pay figures are estimates: check them against your pay stubs and statements. Prices may be delayed or unavailable. Savings ideas are suggestions based on categories you entered, not verified prices. Shiftwise does not move money, trade, or connect to bank accounts.

Downloaded apps (Android and Linux)

  • Everything stays on your device. There is no Shiftwise account, sign-in or server, and nothing syncs between devices.
  • Your records are encrypted with your vault passphrase. The passphrase cannot be reset. If you forget it, the records cannot be opened.
  • Removing the app, clearing its storage or losing the device removes your records. Export an encrypted backup from Pay settings now and then. On Android, the app opts out of cloud backup, so Google does not keep a copy.
  • Live crypto prices are off until you turn them on in Appearance & app. When on, only coin symbols such as BTC are sent to Coinbase's public price service, which sees your internet address like any website.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking, crash reports or automatic update checks. No camera, microphone, location or contacts access.
  • Shop and offer links open in your normal browser, where that site's own policies apply.

Website version

On the website, records are encrypted in your browser before syncing. The hosting service receives encrypted records, account identifiers, versions and request information, but not your passphrase. Crypto price requests pass through the website's server, which sends only coin symbols to Coinbase. See the full privacy notice and security notes.

Age

Creating a vault requires confirming you are 18 or older. The age you enter is checked on your device and is not stored. This is a self-declaration, not identity verification.

Security limits

Encryption protects stored records. It cannot protect an unlocked screen, a device with malware, or someone who knows your passphrase. Shiftwise locks after 5 minutes without use, or 1 minute in the background. No independent security audit has been done.
