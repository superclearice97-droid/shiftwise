# Release legal status and owner actions

This is a technical release review, not legal advice, a legal opinion, or a compliance certification.

## Addressed in v0.1.0

- Bundled Three.js and font notices retained in source and compiled release packages.
- No unlicensed Factory Yard source imported.
- Privacy/data and financial limitation notices included and linked from the app.
- Existing adult self-declaration, self-hosted fonts, encryption and reduced-motion controls retained.
- No advertising session replay, app-owned marketing emails or subscriptions are implemented, so this version does not invent consent, unsubscribe addresses or renewal terms for nonexistent flows.
- No production records or credentials included in the repository or release packages.
- Original-code license remains ungranted; no broad reuse rights or owner's legal name were invented.

## Not completed; owner action required

1. Confirm the legal operator's full name and jurisdiction, and establish a private privacy/security contact. The earlier choice to operate under your own legal name did not supply verified registration details.
2. Establish account deletion, retention/backups and incident-response processes with the host. Current entry deletion is not a complete account-erasure process.
3. Decide whether to grant an open-source or other distribution license for original Shiftwise code. Third-party licenses remain in effect independently.
4. Assess applicable privacy/consumer and financial-data laws for actual users and operations with qualified counsel before broader onboarding. A financial disclaimer alone does not resolve regulatory obligations.
5. DMCA agent registration has NOT been submitted. The Copyright Office describes it as part of eligibility for relevant section 512 safe harbors, not a universal app-publishing registration. If appropriate for the service, the owner must register, pay the fee, and maintain matching public agent information and notice procedures. Do not describe this app as DMCA-registered.
6. App-store publication and signed Android/iOS/Linux installers are not part of this source/web-build release. No trademark clearance or independent security audit has been completed.
